Lock Our Photos 🔒

This page turns your romantic photos into one locked file. Everything happens on your own device; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1Choose your romantic photos (and short videos)

2Choose a password

⚠️ If you forget this password, the folder cannot be opened, not even by you. You would simply lock the photos again with a new password.

3Lock and download

You'll get a file called vault.enc. Put it in the website folder at assets/private/vault.enc (replace the old one if there is one).
Keep your original photos on your phone or computer, not in the website folder.
